I'm Luigi, an Information Technology student at the University of San Carlos in Cebu, with hands-on time in networking, cybersecurity fundamentals and software development.

          
Most of that came from Cisco Networking Academy labs: subnetting by hand, configuring switches and routers in simulated enterprise networks, and learning how analysts spot and respond to threats. The rest came from projects I built because I wanted to see if I could.

          
I'm heading into IT support, where the job is to keep people's tech out of their way. That means configuring the switch, triaging the ticket, and explaining the fix in plain words.

          
Off the clock I make games in Godot and apps with too many features. My favourite conditions: late-night playlists and rain outside.
